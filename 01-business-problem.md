# 01 — Business Problem

## 1. Business Context

GlobalFawusi is a business consulting and growth strategy company focused on helping entrepreneurs and MSMEs build better-structured, sustainable businesses.

The company supports businesses across areas including business strategy, planning, growth assessment, customer acquisition, business development, practical advisory support, training, and business education.

## 2. Current Situation

As GlobalFawusi develops its consulting and business-growth services, there is an opportunity to create a more structured approach to understanding the needs of entrepreneurs and MSMEs.

Different businesses may experience different challenges depending on their stage of development, industry, customer base, operational structure, and growth objectives.

A structured business analysis approach can help GlobalFawusi identify recurring challenges, organize client needs, and develop more consistent recommendations and support solutions.

## 3. Business Problem

GlobalFawusi needs a structured framework for assessing the challenges, needs, and growth opportunities of entrepreneurs and MSMEs so that consulting recommendations can be more consistent, evidence-based, and actionable.

## 4. Problem Statement

> How can GlobalFawusi create a structured business analysis framework that helps identify the key challenges and growth needs of entrepreneurs and MSMEs and translate those findings into practical consulting recommendations?

## 5. Business Impact

Without a structured approach to assessing business needs, several challenges may arise:

- Inconsistent assessment of client problems
- Difficulty identifying recurring business challenges
- Recommendations that may not be sufficiently evidence-based
- Limited ability to compare businesses across different areas
- Difficulty tracking whether recommended interventions address the underlying problem
- Reduced scalability of the consulting process

## 6. Key Business Question

The central business question for this project is:

> What framework can GlobalFawusi use to systematically assess business challenges, identify growth opportunities, and recommend appropriate interventions for entrepreneurs and MSMEs?

## 7. Project Objective

The objective of this project is to design a practical business analysis framework that GlobalFawusi can use to:

1. Understand the current state of a business.
2. Identify key business problems and their root causes.
3. Analyze business performance and growth opportunities.
4. Prioritize the most important issues.
5. Develop practical recommendations.
6. Create a foundation for measuring improvement over time.

## 8. Expected Outcome

The expected outcome is a repeatable business analysis process that can be used as part of GlobalFawusi's consulting methodology.

The framework should help move the consulting process from:

**Problem → Analysis → Insight → Recommendation → Action → Measurement**

rather than relying primarily on informal assessment or intuition.
