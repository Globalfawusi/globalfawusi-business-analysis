# 02 — Stakeholder Analysis

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of the stakeholder analysis is to identify the individuals and groups affected by GlobalFawusi's business analysis framework, understand their interests and needs, and determine how they should be engaged throughout the consulting process.

> Note: Stakeholder roles and needs in this case study are based on the project's defined business model and reasonable case-study assumptions. They are not presented as findings from actual client interviews.

## 2. Key Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Role | Interest | Influence | Key Needs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GlobalFawusi Founder / Lead Consultant | Project owner and decision-maker | High | High | Clear framework, actionable insights, scalable consulting process |
| Consulting / Advisory Team | Delivers analysis and recommendations | High | High | Standardized methodology, clear requirements, reliable information |
| Entrepreneurs / MSME Clients | Primary beneficiaries of the service | High | Medium | Clarity, practical recommendations, business growth support |
| Client Business Owners / Decision-Makers | Provide information and approve actions | High | High | Useful insights, prioritized recommendations, measurable outcomes |
| Training Participants | Receive business education and support | Medium | Low | Practical knowledge, tools, and guidance |
| Potential Partners | May support referrals, training, funding, or business development | Medium | Medium | Clear value proposition and defined collaboration opportunities |

## 3. Stakeholder Needs

### GlobalFawusi Founder / Lead Consultant

The founder needs a structured framework that can:

- Standardize how businesses are assessed.
- Improve the quality and consistency of consulting recommendations.
- Identify recurring business problems.
- Support evidence-based decision-making.
- Make the consulting process easier to scale.
- Provide measurable outcomes for clients.

### Consulting / Advisory Team

The consulting team needs:

- A clear assessment process.
- Standardized questions and information requirements.
- Defined analysis steps.
- Consistent documentation.
- A framework for prioritizing business problems.
- Clear criteria for developing recommendations.

### Entrepreneurs / MSME Clients

Clients need:

- A clear understanding of their current business position.
- Identification of major business challenges.
- Practical recommendations rather than purely theoretical advice.
- Prioritization of the issues that matter most.
- Guidance on implementation.
- A way to measure progress.

### Client Business Owners / Decision-Makers

Business owners need:

- Reliable information about business performance.
- Clear explanations of identified problems.
- Evidence supporting recommendations.
- Understanding of the expected business impact.
- Practical next steps.

### Training Participants

Training participants need:

- Accessible business knowledge.
- Practical tools and frameworks.
- Examples they can apply to their businesses.
- Guidance for making better business decisions.

### Potential Partners

Potential partners need:

- A clear understanding of GlobalFawusi's value proposition.
- Defined areas for collaboration.
- Evidence of the company's consulting and business-development capabilities.
- Clear mutual benefits.

## 4. Stakeholder Power / Interest Matrix

### High Influence / High Interest

**GlobalFawusi Founder / Lead Consultant**

**Client Business Owners / Decision-Makers**

These stakeholders should be closely managed because they have significant influence over project direction, decisions, and implementation.

### High Influence / Medium Interest

**Potential Partners**

These stakeholders should be kept satisfied and engaged when their involvement is relevant to the project.

### Medium Influence / High Interest

**Entrepreneurs / MSME Clients**

These stakeholders should be kept closely informed because the framework is designed primarily around their needs.

### High Interest / Lower Influence

**Consulting / Advisory Team**

These stakeholders should be actively involved in the design and use of the framework because they will apply the process in practice.

### Lower Influence / Medium Interest

**Training Participants**

These stakeholders should be kept informed and may provide useful feedback about the clarity and usefulness of GlobalFawusi's educational resources.

## 5. Stakeholder Engagement Approach

| Stakeholder | Engagement Approach |
|---|---|
| Founder / Lead Consultant | Regular review and decision-making |
| Consulting / Advisory Team | Workshops, process reviews, and feedback |
| Entrepreneurs / MSME Clients | Interviews, assessments, feedback, and consultations |
| Business Owners / Decision-Makers | Requirements gathering, validation, and solution approval |
| Training Participants | Surveys, feedback, and learning assessments |
| Potential Partners | Structured discussions and partnership reviews |

## 6. Key Stakeholder Insight

The analysis indicates that the most important stakeholders for the proposed framework are the **GlobalFawusi consulting team and the entrepreneurs/MSME clients**.

The framework therefore needs to balance two priorities:

1. **Internal consistency** — ensuring consultants have a repeatable method for assessing businesses.
2. **Client usefulness** — ensuring the resulting analysis produces practical recommendations that business owners can understand and act upon.

This balance will guide the requirements and solution design in the next stages of the project.
