04 — Current-State Process

1. Purpose

This document models the current-state consulting journey for GlobalFawusi at a high level.

The purpose is to understand how a prospective entrepreneur or MSME may move from initial contact through assessment, advisory support, recommendations, and follow-up.

Note: This is a high-level case-study model based on GlobalFawusi’s stated services. It is not presented as a documented internal operating procedure.

2. Current-State Process Overview

The current-state journey can be represented as:

Awareness / Referral → Initial Contact → Business Discussion → Needs Assessment → Advisory / Analysis → Recommendations → Implementation Support → Follow-Up

3. Process Stages

Stage 1 — Awareness / Referral

A potential client becomes aware of GlobalFawusi through channels such as:

* Referrals
* Networking
* Online presence
* Business education
* Direct outreach
* Existing relationships

Output: Potential client identified.

Stage 2 — Initial Contact

The entrepreneur or business owner contacts GlobalFawusi or is contacted through an appropriate channel.

Initial information may be gathered about:

* Business type
* Current situation
* General needs
* Reason for seeking support

Output: Initial understanding of the client’s situation.

Stage 3 — Business Discussion

A more detailed conversation is used to understand the business and the reason for seeking support.

Potential areas of discussion include:

* Business goals
* Current challenges
* Growth plans
* Customer-related issues
* Operational concerns
* Strategic concerns

Output: Initial problem areas identified.

Stage 4 — Needs Assessment

The business is assessed to understand its current position and identify the areas requiring attention.

Potential assessment areas include:

* Strategy
* Marketing
* Customer acquisition
* Sales
* Operations
* Finance
* Human resources
* Technology
* Business development

Output: Identified business needs and potential problem areas.

Stage 5 — Advisory / Analysis

The identified issues are examined to understand their significance and potential causes.

Activities may include:

* Reviewing available information
* Analyzing business performance
* Identifying gaps
* Exploring possible causes
* Assessing growth opportunities

Output: Business insights and prioritized areas for attention.

Stage 6 — Recommendations

Recommendations are developed based on the identified problems and opportunities.

Recommendations should ideally include:

* Problem being addressed
* Proposed action
* Priority
* Expected outcome
* Relevant performance indicator

Output: Recommended actions.

Stage 7 — Implementation Support

Where appropriate, GlobalFawusi provides practical advisory or support to help the business implement recommended actions.

Output: Actions initiated by the business.

Stage 8 — Follow-Up

Progress may be reviewed to determine whether recommended actions are producing the intended results.

Potential areas of review include:

* Progress against objectives
* Performance indicators
* New challenges
* Implementation barriers
* Additional support requirements

Output: Updated business insights and potential next steps.

5. Potential Pain Points

Because this is a case-study model rather than an audited internal process, the following are treated as areas for investigation, not confirmed problems.

Pain Point 1 — Inconsistent Information Collection

Different businesses may provide different levels or types of information during the initial assessment.

Potential impact: Difficulty comparing businesses or identifying patterns across engagements.

Pain Point 2 — Unstructured Problem Identification

Business owners may describe symptoms rather than the underlying problem.

Potential impact: Recommendations may address symptoms instead of root causes.

Pain Point 3 — Limited Standardization

Without a repeatable assessment framework, the consulting process may depend heavily on the individual consultant’s approach.

Potential impact: Inconsistent analysis and recommendations.

Pain Point 4 — Difficulty Prioritizing Problems

A business may have several problems simultaneously.

Potential impact: Time and resources may be spent on issues that are less important than higher-impact problems.

Pain Point 5 — Limited Measurement

Recommendations may not always be connected to clearly defined KPIs or measurable outcomes.

Potential impact: Difficulty determining whether an intervention produced the intended result.

6. Process Improvement Opportunity

The current-state model suggests an opportunity to introduce a more structured assessment and analysis layer between Business Discussion and Recommendations.

The proposed improvement is:

Business Discussion → Structured Assessment → Data Analysis → Root-Cause Analysis → Prioritization → Recommendations

This will be explored further in the solution-design stage.

7. Key Process Insight

The most important opportunity identified at this stage is the potential to make the consulting process more repeatable, measurable, and data-informed without removing the practical and personalized nature of GlobalFawusi’s advisory approach.
