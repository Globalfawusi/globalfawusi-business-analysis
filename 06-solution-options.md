06 — Solution Options

1. Purpose

This document evaluates potential solutions for improving GlobalFawusi’s business assessment, analysis, recommendation, and follow-up process.

The solutions are evaluated against the problems and requirements identified in the previous stages of the case study.

2. Solution Design Principles

Any proposed solution should:

* Improve consistency without eliminating customization.
* Make business assessments easier to structure.
* Support evidence-based decision-making.
* Allow problems to be prioritized.
* Connect recommendations to measurable outcomes.
* Be practical for entrepreneurs and MSMEs.
* Be scalable as GlobalFawusi grows.
* Protect client information and maintain appropriate data privacy.

3. Solution Option 1 — Standardized Business Assessment Framework

Description

Develop a structured assessment framework that consultants can use when evaluating entrepreneurs and MSMEs.

The framework would include:

* Standardized business profile
* Business health assessment
* Challenge identification
* Growth-objective assessment
* Root-cause analysis
* Problem prioritization
* Recommendation template
* KPI and follow-up framework

Advantages

* Relatively simple to implement.
* Creates consistency across assessments.
* Requires limited technology investment initially.
* Can be adapted to different industries.
* Creates a foundation for future digital tools.

Disadvantages

* May remain heavily dependent on manual processes.
* Data analysis may still require separate tools.
* Reporting and trend analysis may be limited.
* Scaling the process could eventually become difficult.

Estimated Complexity

Low

Expected Impact

Medium to High

4. Solution Option 2 — Digital Business Assessment & Analytics System

Description

Develop a digital system that allows business owners or consultants to capture business information through a structured assessment.

The system could eventually include:

* Digital business assessment forms
* Centralized business profiles
* Automated scoring
* Business health indicators
* Challenge categorization
* Recommendation tracking
* KPI tracking
* Analytics dashboards
* Historical assessment records

Advantages

* Creates a centralized source of business information.
* Improves data consistency.
* Enables analysis across multiple businesses.
* Supports automated reporting.
* Provides a foundation for scaling GlobalFawusi’s consulting platform.
* Could eventually support data-driven benchmarking.

Disadvantages

* Requires greater development effort.
* Requires technical resources.
* Requires stronger data governance.
* Higher initial cost.
* Requires ongoing maintenance.

Estimated Complexity

High

Expected Impact

High

5. Solution Option 3 — Hybrid Consulting & Analytics Model

Description

Combine a standardized consulting framework with digital data-analysis tools.

The consulting methodology would remain human-led while technology supports information collection, analysis, reporting, and follow-up.

A simplified model would be:

Assessment → Data Collection → Analysis → Consultant Review → Recommendation → Implementation → KPI Tracking

Technology could initially include tools such as:

* Structured digital forms
* Excel
* SQL
* Power BI
* Centralized documentation

More advanced software could be introduced later as GlobalFawusi grows.

Advantages

* Combines human consulting expertise with data analysis.
* Can be implemented progressively.
* Lower initial complexity than building a complete software platform.
* Creates a pathway toward future digital transformation.
* Allows GlobalFawusi to test the methodology before investing heavily in custom technology.
* Supports both personalized consulting and standardized analysis.

Disadvantages

* Requires consultants to consistently use the framework.
* Some processes may remain partially manual.
* Multiple tools may need to be maintained.
* Full automation would require future development.

Estimated Complexity

Medium

Expected Impact

High

Scoring interpretation: High Score represents a more favorable position for the criterion. For cost and complexity, a high score means lower cost or lower complexity.

7. Recommended Solution

Recommendation: Option 3 — Hybrid Consulting & Analytics Model

The hybrid model is recommended because it provides the strongest balance between immediate practicality and long-term scalability.

Rather than attempting to build a complete technology platform immediately, GlobalFawusi can first establish a standardized consulting methodology and support it with accessible analytical tools.

The approach can then evolve as the business grows.

8. Proposed Implementation Path

Phase 1 — Standardize

Develop:

* Business assessment framework
* Requirements structure
* Problem-prioritization method
* Recommendation framework
* KPI framework

Phase 2 — Digitize

Introduce:

* Digital assessment forms
* Structured data storage
* Excel-based analysis
* SQL-based analysis where appropriate
* Power BI dashboards

Phase 3 — Scale

As usage increases, evaluate the need for:

* Custom software
* Client portals
* Automated business scoring
* Automated reporting
* Business benchmarking
* Integrated CRM and client-management systems

9. Expected Business Benefits

The recommended approach could help GlobalFawusi:

* Improve consistency across consulting engagements.
* Reduce dependence on unstructured information.
* Make recommendations more evidence-based.
* Identify recurring business challenges.
* Track client progress more effectively.
* Generate reusable business insights.
* Build a foundation for future digital products.
* Scale its consulting methodology more efficiently.

10. Key Recommendation

GlobalFawusi should standardize the consulting methodology first, digitize the supporting processes second, and develop more advanced technology as validated demand and scale justify the investment.

This reduces implementation risk while creating a clear path from a traditional consulting model toward a data-driven business growth platform.
