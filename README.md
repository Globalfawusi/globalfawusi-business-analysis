GlobalFawusi — Business Growth & Performance Analysis

Project Overview

GlobalFawusi is a business consulting and growth strategy company focused on helping entrepreneurs and MSMEs build better-structured, sustainable businesses.

The company provides support across business strategy, planning, growth assessment, customer acquisition, business development, practical advisory services, training, and business education.

This case study explores how business analysis and data-driven decision-making can be used to help GlobalFawusi better understand the needs of entrepreneurs and MSMEs and improve how growth support can be structured and delivered.

Business Problem

As GlobalFawusi develops its consulting and entrepreneurship platform, there is an opportunity to create a more structured approach to understanding the businesses it serves.

The key business question for this project is:

How can GlobalFawusi use business analysis and data to better understand the challenges, needs, and growth priorities of entrepreneurs and MSMEs, and use those insights to design more effective support?

The analysis will examine areas such as:

* Business stage and characteristics
* Common business challenges
* Growth objectives
* Customer acquisition needs
* Operational and strategic needs
* Potential areas of demand for GlobalFawusi’s services

Project Objective

The objective of this project is to develop a data-informed framework that can help GlobalFawusi:

1. Understand the business needs of its target market.
2. Identify common challenges and growth priorities.
3. Segment businesses according to relevant characteristics and needs.
4. Identify opportunities for improving its consulting and support offerings.
5. Develop practical recommendations based on the analysis.

Analyst Role

Role: Business Analyst & Growth Strategy Analyst

The project will follow a business analysis process from:

Business Problem → Stakeholder Analysis → Requirements → Data → Analysis → Insights → Recommendations

Data Note

Where real client or company data is not available, this project will use clearly labelled simulated data created for portfolio and analytical demonstration purposes.

No simulated data will be presented as actual GlobalFawusi client information.
