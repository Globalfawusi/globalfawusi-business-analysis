03 — Requirements

1. Purpose

This document defines the business and functional requirements for the proposed GlobalFawusi business analysis framework.

The requirements translate stakeholder needs into specific capabilities that the framework should provide.

Note: These requirements are developed for this portfolio case study based on the defined business problem and stakeholder analysis. They are not presented as requirements formally gathered from actual GlobalFawusi clients.

2. Business Requirements

BR-01 — Standardized Business Assessment

The framework should provide a standardized method for assessing the current state of an entrepreneur’s or MSME’s business.

BR-02 — Business Problem Identification

The framework should help identify and document the major challenges affecting a business.

BR-03 — Root-Cause Identification

The framework should help distinguish between symptoms and underlying causes of identified business problems.

BR-04 — Business Prioritization

The framework should allow identified problems and opportunities to be prioritized according to factors such as business impact, urgency, and feasibility.

BR-05 — Growth Opportunity Identification

The framework should help identify potential opportunities for business growth, improvement, and development.

BR-06 — Recommendation Development

The framework should support the development of practical, evidence-based recommendations linked to identified business problems.

BR-07 — Action Planning

The framework should translate recommendations into clear actions, priorities, and implementation steps.

BR-08 — Performance Measurement

The framework should define measurable indicators that can be used to evaluate progress after recommendations are implemented.

BR-09 — Scalability

The framework should be structured in a way that allows GlobalFawusi to apply it consistently across different entrepreneurs, startups, and MSMEs.

3. Functional Requirements

FR-01 — Business Profile

The framework should capture relevant information about the business, including:

* Industry
* Business stage
* Business size
* Products or services
* Target customers
* Revenue range
* Growth objectives

FR-02 — Challenge Assessment

The framework should capture the major challenges identified by the business.

Potential categories may include:

* Strategy
* Marketing
* Customer acquisition
* Sales
* Operations
* Finance
* Human resources
* Technology
* Business development

FR-03 — Growth Objective Assessment

The framework should capture the business’s primary growth objectives.

Examples may include:

* Increasing revenue
* Acquiring more customers
* Improving profitability
* Expanding into new markets
* Improving operations
* Launching new products or services
* Building organizational capacity

FR-04 — Problem Prioritization

The framework should allow identified problems to be ranked according to defined criteria.

Possible criteria include:

* Business impact
* Urgency
* Difficulty
* Cost
* Potential return

FR-05 — Recommendation Tracking

The framework should document:

* Identified problem
* Recommended action
* Responsible party
* Priority
* Expected outcome
* Relevant KPI

FR-06 — KPI Tracking

The framework should support tracking of relevant performance indicators over time.

4. Non-Functional Requirements

NFR-01 — Usability

The framework should be simple enough for consultants and business owners to understand and use without extensive technical knowledge.

NFR-02 — Consistency

The same core assessment methodology should be applicable across different businesses.

NFR-03 — Maintainability

The framework should be easy to update as GlobalFawusi’s consulting methodology evolves.

NFR-04 — Scalability

The framework should support increasing numbers of business assessments without requiring a complete redesign of the process.

NFR-05 — Data Privacy

Client information should be handled responsibly and should not be exposed publicly without appropriate authorization.

5. Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful if the resulting framework enables GlobalFawusi to:

1. Assess businesses using a consistent methodology.
2. Clearly document identified business problems.
3. Prioritize problems according to defined criteria.
4. Connect recommendations to specific business needs.
5. Define measurable outcomes for recommended actions.
6. Reuse the framework across different businesses.
7. Generate structured information that can later be analyzed using data-analysis tools.

6. Assumptions

The case study assumes that:

* Businesses are willing to provide relevant information about their operations.
* The information provided is sufficiently accurate for analysis.
* Different businesses may have different priorities and challenges.
* The framework will initially be used by GlobalFawusi’s consulting/advisory function.
* The dataset used later in this portfolio will be simulated unless explicitly identified otherwise.

7. Constraints

Potential constraints include:

* Limited availability of reliable business data.
* Differences in how businesses measure performance.
* Limited resources available to small businesses.
* Varying levels of business-management maturity.
* Data privacy and confidentiality considerations.
